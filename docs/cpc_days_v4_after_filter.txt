**Short answer:** The Registrar of the Central Processing Centre must decide on an application, e-form, or document within thirty days from the date of its filing [S1].

**Details:**
*   This timeline excludes cases where approval from the Central Government, Regional Director, or any other competent authority is required [S1].
*   This rule came into force on February 16, 2024 [S1].

**Reported experience (if any):** None found.
