**Short answer:** A reserved company name can be extended for up to 60 days from the date of approval, with varying fees depending on the extension period chosen [S4].

**Details:**
*   The Registrar can extend the period of a reserved name using the web service SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically Plus: INC-32) [S4].
*   The extension options and associated fees (date_retrieved: 2026-11-02) are:
    *   Up to 40 days from the date of approval, on payment of a fee of one thousand rupees, if made before the expiry of 20 days from the date of approval [S4].
    *   Up to 60 days from the date of approval, on payment of a fee of two thousand rupees, if made before the expiry of 40 days from the date of approval (as referred to in the 40-day extension clause) [S4].
    *   Up to 60 days from the date of approval, on payment of a fee of three thousand rupees, if made before the expiry of 20 days from the date of approval [S4].
*   The Registrar has the power to cancel a reserved name in accordance with sub-section (5) of section 4 of the Companies Act [S4].
