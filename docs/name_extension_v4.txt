**Short answer:** A reserved company name for a proposed company can be extended up to a total of 60 days from the date of approval, with varying fees depending on the extension period and when the fee is paid [S3].

**Details:**
*   Initially, a name for a proposed company is reserved for a period of twenty days from the date of approval [S1].
*   The reservation period can be extended as follows [S3] (date_retrieved: 2026-11-02):
    *   **Up to forty days** from the date of approval, by paying a fee of **rupees one thousand** before the expiry of the initial twenty days from the date of approval [S3].
    *   **Up to sixty days** from the date of approval, by paying a fee of **rupees two thousand** before the expiry of the forty-day period mentioned above [S3].
    *   Alternatively, **up to sixty days** from the date of approval, by paying a fee of **rupees three thousand** before the expiry of the initial twenty days from the date of approval [S3].
