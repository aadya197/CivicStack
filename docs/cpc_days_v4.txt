**Short answer:** The Registrar of the Central Processing Center must decide on an application, e-form, or document within thirty days from the date of its filing [S1].

**Details:**
*   This timeline excludes cases where approval from the Central Government, Regional Director, or any other competent authority is required [S1].
*   These rules came into force on February 16, 2024 [S1].
*   The Central Processing Center Registrar has jurisdiction all over India for the examination of specific applications, e-Forms, or documents, including [S1]:
    *   Filing of Resolutions and agreements under section 117 (e-Form no. MGT-14) [S1].
    *   Notice to Registrar of any alteration of share capital under section 64 (e-Form no. SH-7) [S1].
    *   Application for approval of Central Government for change of name under section 13 (e-Form no. INC-24) [S1].
    *   One Person Company - Application for Conversion under section 18 (e-Form no. INC-6) [S1].
    *   Conversion of public company into private company or private company into public company under sections 14 and 18 (e-Form no. INC-27) [S1].
    *   Intimation to Registrar of revocation/surrender of license issued [S1].
