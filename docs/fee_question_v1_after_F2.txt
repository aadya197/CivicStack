The exact government fee for registering a Private Limited Company in India is not a single, fixed amount. It depends on a few key factors, primarily the **state where the company is registered** (due to varying stamp duty) and the **authorized share capital** of the company.

However, I can break down the components of the government fees for you:

Here are the typical government fees as of today (these rates are generally stable but can be subject to minor changes by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs - MCA):

1.  **Name Reservation (RUN Form):**
    *   **Fee:** ₹1,000
    *   **Purpose:** To reserve the name of your company. This fee is payable if you file the RUN (Reserve Unique Name) form separately. If you file SPICe+ Part A (for name reservation) along with SPICe+ Part B (for incorporation), this fee is effectively bundled.

2.  **SPICe+ Part B (Incorporation Form):**
    *   **Fee:** ₹500
    *   **Purpose:** This is the filing fee for the main incorporation application, which includes the e-MOA (Memorandum of Association) and e-AOA (Articles of Association).

3.  **Fees for Authorized Capital (ROC Fees):**
    *   This fee is calculated based on the authorized share capital of the company.
