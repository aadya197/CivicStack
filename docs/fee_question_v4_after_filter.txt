**Short answer:** I could not find a definitive exact government fee for registering a Private Limited company in the available sources due to conflicting information from practitioner sources. You should verify the current fees on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal before relying on any figures.

**Details:**
*   A practitioner source reports MCA filing fees for company incorporation, retrieved on 2026-10-29 [S3]. These fees vary based on authorized capital:
    *   Up to ₹1 lakh capital: ₹500 [S3]
    *   ₹1 lakh to ₹5 lakh capital: ₹2,000 [S3]
    *   ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh capital: ₹5,000 [S3]
    *   ₹10 lakh to ₹50 lakh capital: ₹10,000 [S3]
    *   Name reservation (Part A) costs ₹1,000, which is included in the filing fee if Part A is used [S3].
*   However, another practitioner source, a hand-transcribed table of fees retrieved on 2026-11-02, states that for companies incorporated with a nominal capital not exceeding rupees ten lakhs, no fee shall be payable for submitting, filing, registering, or recording any document, effective from 26.01.2018 [S4].
