===== Q13 v4 (V4.3) | sources: 8
**Short answer:** A Private Limited company is not allowed to have unlimited public shareholders. The Companies Act, 2013, as amended up to 1 April 2021, specifies that a private company limits the number of its members to two hundred and prohibits any invitation to the public to subscribe for its securities [S8].

**Details:**
*   A private company, except for a One Person Company, limits the number of its members to two hundred [S8].
*   Persons who hold shares jointly are treated as a single member for this limit [S8].
*   Current and former employees who were members while employed and continue to be members after employment ceases are not included in this count [S8].
*   A private company prohibits any invitation to the public to subscribe for its securities [S8].

===== Q14 v4 (V4.3) | sources: 8
**Short answer:** A reserved company name, initially approved for 20 days, can be extended up to a total of 60 days from the date of approval, with fees varying based on the extension period chosen [S1, S7].

**Details:**
*   **Initial Reservation:** A name for a proposed company is reserved for a period of twenty days from the date of approval [S1].
*   **Extension Options (fees retrieved 2026-11-02):**
    *   **To 40 days:** The reservation can be extended up to forty days from the date of approval by paying a fee of one thousand rupees. This payment must be made before the expiry of the initial twenty days from the date of approval [S7].
    *   **To 60 days (Option 1):** The reservation can be extended up to sixty days from the date of approval by paying a fee of two thousand rupees. This payment must be made before the expiry of the forty-day period (if the 40-day extension was previously taken) [S7].
    *   **To 60 days (Option 2):** Alternatively, the reservation can be extended directly up to sixty days from the date of approval by paying a fee of three thousand rupees. This payment must be made before the expiry of the initial twenty days from the date of approval [S7].
*   These extensions are processed through the web service available at www.mca.gov.in, using SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically Plus: INC-32) [S7].
*   The Registrar has the power to cancel a reserved name if it was applied for by furnishing wrong or incorrect information [S1, S7].
