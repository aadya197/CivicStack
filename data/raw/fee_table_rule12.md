# Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014: Rule 12 and Annexure, Table of Fees (compiled copy, hand-transcribed)

SOURCE NOTE: Transcribed by hand from page images of a compiled copy of Rule 12 and its Annexure. This is NOT the official Gazette text. Where the copy prints "---" the cell has no amount printed, and that is kept as "---" here. The copy carries amendment footnotes up to 2020. Later amendments exist, including G.S.R. 300(E) of 21 April 2026, which replaced the DIR-3 KYC fee (see item VII). Verify any figure against the Gazette notifications on the MCA portal before relying on it. Amounts are in rupees.

Rule 12 (Fees), summary: (1) Documents required to be submitted, filed, registered or recorded under the Act are filed on payment of the fee or additional fee in the Table annexed to the rules. (2) Where no e-form is prescribed, the document or application is filed through Form GNL-1 or GNL-2 with the applicable fee, and if a single form is prescribed for multiple purposes the fee is paid for each purpose. (3) Information under section 2(60) of the Act is filed in Form GNL-3 with the applicable fee.

#### Notes to Table A (as printed)
1. The table for small companies and one person companies applies provided the company remains in that class for a period of not less than one year from incorporation.
2. The table applies to intimation to the Registrar under section 159, filing of notice of appointment of auditors, Secretarial Auditor or Cost Auditor.
3. The table and calculation for increase in authorised capital apply to revised capital under section 233(11) of the Act (after setting off fee paid by the transferor company on its authorised capital before merger or amalgamation).
4. The table applies to filing a revised financial statement or board report under sections 130 and 131.

### B. Additional fee for delay in filing forms other than increase in nominal share capital, or forms under section 92 / 137
- Up to 15 days (sections 139 and 157): one time the normal filing fee
- More than 15 days and up to 30 days (sections 139 and 157), and up to 30 days for remaining forms: 2 times normal filing fees
- More than 30 days and up to 60 days: 4 times
- More than 60 days and up to 90 days: 6 times
- More than 90 days and up to 180 days: 10 times
- Beyond 180 days: 12 times
- Note: belated filings that were due before notification of these fee rules attract the fee applicable at the time of actual filing.

### C. Increase in authorised capital: additional fee for delay
- Delay up to 6 months: 2.5% per month on the fees payable under para I.3 or II.12 of Table A
- Delay beyond 6 months: 3% per month on the fees payable under para I.3 or II.12 of Table A
- Also applicable for delay in filing an application with the Registrar under section 233(11).

### D. Forms under section 92 or 137 (annual return, financial statements)
(i) Where the period for submitting a document under section 92 or 137 expires after 30/06/2018, the additional fee is: delay beyond the period under section 92(4): One Hundred rupees per day; delay beyond the period under section 137 (printed in the copy as "737(t)"): One Hundred rupees per day.
(ii) In all other cases of belated annual returns or financial statements due under the 1956 or 2013 Act: up to 30 days: 2 times normal fees; more than 30 days and up to 60 days: 4 times; more than 60 and up to 90 days: 6 times; more than 90 and up to 180 days: 10 times; beyond 180 days: 12 times. The additional fee also applies to revised financial statements or board report under sections 130 and 131 and to secretarial audit reports filed under section 204.
